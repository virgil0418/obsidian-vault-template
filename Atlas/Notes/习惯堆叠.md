习惯堆叠，就是把要养成的新习惯和已有的习惯绑定。

> [!NOTE] 公式
> “After CURRENT HABIT, I will NEW HABIT.”

> [!red] [[习惯堆叠|Habit stacking]]+[[诱惑捆绑|Temptation Bundling]]公⁠式: 
> After \[CURRENT HABIT\], I will \[HABIT I NEED\]. After \[HABIT I NEED\], I will \[HABIT I WANT\].
