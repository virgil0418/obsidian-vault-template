Show what you are doing with the system, don't just show the system. Everyone can show their own systems, but only the systems that really works are valuable. If you are not making any progress with the system, there's no reason for showing it. [[The loudest person in the room is the weakest]].

也就是说在展示我们的系统时，要同时展示这个系统带来的结果。在这个基础上，再去展示自己的系统，因为[[比起结果，人们更想要知道你是怎么做的，你的过程。]]
