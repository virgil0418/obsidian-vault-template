> [!info] Echelon Form
> > **pivot（主元）**: The first non-zero entry of a row
> - 全零行一定在最后
> - 下面的pivot一定在上面的pivot右边
> - pivot下面都是0
> ![[image-1767755002976.webp|454x177]]

> [!NOTE] Reduced Echelon Form
> • 每个pivot都是1
> • 每个pivot都是一列中唯一的非零数字
> ![[image-1767755372822.webp|483x172]]

> [!tip]
> Each [[Matrix]] is row equivalent to **one and only one** reduced echelon matrix
